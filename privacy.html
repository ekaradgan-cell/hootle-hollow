Privacy Policy

Application: Hootle Hollow Uploader
Operator: the owner of the Hootle Hollow YouTube channel (youtube.com/@HootleHollow)
Last updated: 6 November 2026

What this application is

Hootle Hollow Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload original animated short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's own statistics for internal weekly reports. It is not offered to the public and no other user can sign in to it.

Data we access

Through the YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's consent, the application uses these permissions: youtube.upload (to upload the channel's own videos), youtube.readonly (to read the channel's own channel and video information) and yt-analytics.readonly (to read the channel's own aggregated analytics such as views and watch time).

The application does not access, collect or store any personal information about viewers, including children. It does not use cookies, tracking, advertising identifiers or third-party analytics.

How data is stored and used

OAuth tokens and aggregated channel statistics are stored only on the owner's private server, with access restricted to the owner. They are used only to upload the channel's videos and to prepare internal reports. This data is never sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and is never used for advertising.

Hootle Hollow Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention and deletion

Aggregated statistics are kept for up to 24 months for reporting and then deleted. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions or on the Google security settings page; after revocation the stored tokens are deleted within 7 days.

Children

Hootle Hollow publishes content made for children aged 3-7 and marks it as made for kids on YouTube. This application never collects personal information from children or any other viewers.
